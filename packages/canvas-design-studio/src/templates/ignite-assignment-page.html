Overview

This is your capstone moment. You’ve refined your idea, pitched it live, and now you’ll craft a polished 5-minute Ignite Talk video — powered by Generative AI tools. Tell the story of your passion project with visuals, voiceover, and music. Fast-paced, fun, and portfolio-worthy.

What is an Ignite Talk?

“Enlighten us, but make it quick.”

Slides advance automatically — you record your narration to match. The format forces clarity: every word counts, every slide must earn its 15 seconds.

Slide Structure (20 Slides)

Slides 2–17

💡 Slides 18–19 are flex — use them for your call to action, contact info, or a final visual.

Tips for Success
→

Story first, polish second. A great narrative beats fancy effects every time.

→

Keep slides readable at speed. 15 seconds goes faster than you think — one idea per slide.

→

Music enhances — it doesn’t perform. Keep it low under your voice.

→

This is LinkedIn-worthy. Make it something you’re proud to share.
